1. Irrigation Workflow

  1. Data Collection: Gather soil moisture levels, weather forecasts, and crop water requirements.
  2. AI Analysis: Use AI models to analyze the collected data and predict irrigation needs.
  3. Advisory Generation: Generate irrigation recommendations based on AI analysis.
  4. Notification: Send irrigation advisory to farmers via SMS or mobile app.
  5. Implementation: Farmers implement the recommended irrigation schedule.

2. Sensors Used

  • Soil Moisture Sensors: Measure the water content in the soil.
  • Temperature Sensors: Monitor the ambient temperature.
  • Humidity Sensors: Measure the moisture level in the air.
  • Rainfall Sensors: Measure the amount of precipitation.

3. Crop Stages

Germination Stage(Days 0 - 45)
Initial sprouting phase requiring light but frequent moisture to support root development.
Tillering Stage(Days 45 - 120)
Shoot multiplication phase. Water stress must be avoided to ensure maximum tiller density.
Grand Growth Stage(Days 120 - 270)
Critical Stage: Period of rapid stalk elongation and biomass accumulation requiring maximum water availability.
Maturity Stage(Days 270 - 360)
Sugar synthesis and sucrose accumulation. Irrigation is gradually withheld prior to harvesting.
